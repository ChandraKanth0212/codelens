# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Critique code and ideas rather than people. Welcome learners, explain feedback clearly, and respect differing experience levels.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, impersonation, publishing private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Keep discussions relevant and do not share private code or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportional and consistent. Report concerns privately to a maintainer through a contact listed on their GitHub profile; do not publish sensitive incident details. Maintainers must not disclose a reporter's identity without consent.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official community spaces.
